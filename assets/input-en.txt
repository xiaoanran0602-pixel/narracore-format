INT. PROJECTION BOOTH - NIGHT

The projector beam cuts through the dark. Dust drifts in the light.
CHANG sits at the last row, holding a faded cinema ticket.

CHANG
(quietly)
If the film reel still exists, it proves everything about that night.

The USHER steps out of the shadows, flashlight on the ticket.

USHER
This ticket expired twenty years ago.

CHANG rises. The beam snaps off.

CUT TO BLACK.
